# Craft rules: advertising (STUB)

> TODO: expand with tested rules and 1-5 anchors for `craft`.

1. **One thought.** A campaign line holds one idea; "and" is a warning.
2. **Product is the hero or the villain, never a bystander.**
3. **Headline + visual = 3.** Neither repeats the other; together they add up to more.
4. **Platform, not one ad.** If it can't produce a second execution, it is an ad, not a campaign.
5. **Brand-blind test.** Cover the logo: could a competitor sign it? Then it is generic.
