## What a "concept" is here

A campaign idea that can produce many executions: `title` (campaign line or
platform name), `logline` (the insight in one sentence), `concept` (the idea,
why it is true for this product and this audience, how it extends across
channels), `sample` (key visual described in words + headline + one concrete
execution).

## Where originality usually lives

- an insight the category avoids saying;
- the product's flaw made into the message;
- a media idea (the placement is the message);
- a participation mechanic people do without being asked.
