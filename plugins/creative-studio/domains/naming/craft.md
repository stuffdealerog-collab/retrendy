# Craft rules: naming (STUB)

> TODO: expand.

1. **Say it on the phone.** If you have to spell it twice, reconsider.
2. **One reading.** Pronounceable one way in the target languages.
3. **Story in a word.** The name implies a story the brand can tell.
4. **Not a description.** Descriptive names are cheap to understand and impossible to own.
