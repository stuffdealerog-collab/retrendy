## What a "concept" is here

A name with its idea: `title` (the name), `logline` (what it evokes in one
line), `concept` (the logic: source, sound, what it says and refuses to say,
how it scales), `sample` (the name in three uses: sign, sentence, URL/handle).

## Where originality usually lives

- a real word from an unexpected field;
- a name that tells a micro-story;
- a sound shape that fits the product's behaviour.
