## What a "concept" is here

A 15-90 second vertical video idea: `title`, `logline` (who does what, where,
in one sentence), `concept` (the hook in the first 2 seconds, the turn, why
someone rewatches or shares), `sample` (a shot list: 3-6 beats with seconds,
on-screen text and sound).

## Where originality usually lives

- the first 1.5 seconds (a frame that cannot be scrolled past without a question);
- format inversion (a familiar format applied to the wrong subject);
- a rewatch mechanic (a detail you only see the second time);
- sound used against the image.
