# Craft rules: short-form video (STUB)

> TODO: expand with tested rules and 1-5 anchors for the `craft` criterion.

1. **Hook in 1.5 s.** The first frame poses a question or shows an anomaly.
2. **One idea per video.** If it needs two sentences to explain, it is two videos.
3. **Readable without sound** (on-screen text) and **better with sound**.
4. **Loop or payoff.** The last frame either pays off the hook or loops into the first.
5. **Makeable.** One phone, one day, no special permissions, unless the brief has a budget.
