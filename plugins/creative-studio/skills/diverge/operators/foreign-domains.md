# Foreign domains deck

Far domains for the outsider branch. Each card lists structural material
(roles, procedures, units, failure modes, jargon) to import. The draw excludes
cards that mention words in the active domain pack's `outsider_exclude` list.

## Cards

### F01 Beekeeping
Queen, workers, drones; swarming when the hive is too full; smoke calms the bees; the waggle dance tells where food is; winter cluster; robbing by other hives; the honey harvest leaves enough to survive.

### F02 Air traffic control
Call signs, readback of every instruction, holding patterns, handover between sectors, separation minima, "say again", emergency squawk 7700, runway incursion.

### F03 Maritime salvage law
Wreck, flotsam and jetsam, salvage reward proportional to risk, abandonment of a vessel, the duty to render assistance, a claim on what the sea returns.

### F04 Restaurant kitchen brigade
Mise en place, "heard!", the pass, ticket rail, 86'd items, the expediter, family meal, the rush, burns nobody mentions.

### F05 Geology and stratigraphy
Layers laid down over time, unconformity (missing time), fossils as evidence, erosion, core samples, the law of superposition.

### F06 Forensic accounting
Paper trail, reconciliation, round numbers that give fraud away, ledgers that almost balance, the audit, the one transaction that does not fit.

### F07 Lighthouse keeping
The light's character (flash pattern), logbook, fog signal, the keeper's isolation, automation that made keepers obsolete, ships that never see the keeper.

### F08 Chess endgames
Zugzwang (every move worsens your position), opposition, the passed pawn, stalemate as a saving resource, the clock running out.

### F09 Bird migration
Flyways, stopover sites, imprinting, navigation by stars and magnetic fields, vagrants blown off course, ringing (banding) to track them.

### F10 Library cataloguing

### F11 Railway signalling

### F12 Fermentation

### F13 Lost and found office

### F14 Mountain rescue

### F15 Tailoring

### F16 Weather forecasting

### F17 Archive conservation

### F18 Customs and border control

### F20 Watchmaking
