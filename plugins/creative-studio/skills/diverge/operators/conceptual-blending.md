# Conceptual blending (method for the outsider branch)

Fauconnier and Turner's conceptual integration: new meaning appears when two
**input spaces** are projected into a **blend** that has structure neither
input had. The outsider imports **structure** (roles, procedures, rules,
failure modes, vocabulary) from a far domain, not surface decoration. "A love
song with bee imagery" is decoration; "a love song structured as a hive
deciding to swarm: the old queen must leave with half the colony" is a blend.

## Procedure

1. **Input 1 = the brief.** Write its frame in one line: roles, goal, typical
   scene, typical emotion.
2. **Input 2 = your drawn foreign domain** (from `foreign-domains.md`).
   List 8-12 of its concrete elements: roles, objects, procedures, units,
   rules, jargon, characteristic failures, rituals. Prefer the ones an
   insider would mention and an outsider would not know.
3. **Generic space.** What abstract structure do both share? (e.g. "a group
   decides who leaves", "a slow process that cannot be rushed", "a signal sent
   with no confirmation").
4. **Cross-mapping.** Map elements one-to-one: who in the brief is the
   beekeeper, what is the smoke, what is the swarm. Write 4-6 mappings.
5. **Blend, then run it** (the three operations):
   - *composition*: put the mapped elements together in one scene;
   - *completion*: let the foreign domain fill gaps with its own logic (what
     happens next in a hive?);
   - *elaboration*: "run the blend": follow the new logic further than is
     comfortable; this is where the emergent idea appears.
6. **Emergent structure.** Name what the blend has that neither input had.
   That sentence is your concept. If you cannot name it, the blend is
   decoration: discard it.
7. **Strip the scaffolding where needed.** The audience does not need to know
   the foreign domain. Keep its vocabulary only where it adds a precise,
   fresh word; keep its structure always.

## Quality checks

- Would an insider of the foreign domain nod at the mapping? (accuracy gives
  specificity)
- Would someone who knows nothing about it still feel the emotion? (the blend
  must work on the brief's terms)
- Is the foreign domain doing work in the structure, or only in the nouns?

## Cards

Foreign domains are in `foreign-domains.md`; two are drawn per run. Use one as
the main input; the second only if the first produces nothing alive, or blend
both for one wild-card concept.
